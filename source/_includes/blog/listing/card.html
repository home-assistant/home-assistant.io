{%- comment -%} A post on the blog's listing pages. The whole card links to the post. Cards come in three sizes, so the page steps down from one wide card to two, then rows of three: featured: the newest post. Image, category, title, description, date and authors. secondary: the next two posts. Image, title, description, date and authors. compact: everything else. Category, title, description, date and authors, with no image. A post shared from another site gets a pill at the top, in place of the category.
