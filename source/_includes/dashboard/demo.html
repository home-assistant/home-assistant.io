{%- comment -%} A live demo of dashboard cards, running against the mocked backend of the Home Assistant demo. The cards come from `demos` in source/_data/dashboard_demos.yml, with the entities their `entity` names, or that are in their `area`, from `entities` there. source/javascripts/dashboard-demo.js loads the demo when it comes near, and adds the cards from the template once they are in view, so their animations play where they can be seen. The demo follows the released frontend, so a card or card feature that is new in the next release shows as an error until then.
