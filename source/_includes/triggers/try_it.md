## Try it yourself

Ready to test this? Go to {% my automations title="**Settings** > **Automations & scenes**" %}, create a new automation, and add this trigger. Save the automation, and then make the trigger react. For example, change the state of the targeted {% term entity %}, turn on a light that the trigger monitors, or change its brightness. To check whether the automation ran, [open its traces](/docs/automation/testing/#viewing-the-traces-of-an-automation).
