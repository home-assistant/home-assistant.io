
Follow these steps to rearrange floors and areas on the built-in dashboards (such as **Overview**, **Lights**, and **Security**).

1. Go to {% my areas title="**Settings** > **Areas, labels & zones**" %}.
2. Rearrange the floors and areas in one of the following ways:
   - Drag and drop the floors and areas.
   - Next to a floor, or next to the **Other areas** section, select **Menu** {% icon "mdi:dots-vertical" %} > **Reorder**.
     - In the dialog, move the floors or areas.
     - Select **Save**.
