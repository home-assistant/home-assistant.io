
Follow these steps to rearrange floors and areas on the built-in dashboards (such as Overview, Lights, Security).

1. Go to {% my areas title="**Settings** > **Areas, labels & zones**" %} and in the top-right corner, select the three dots {% icon "mdi:dots-vertical" %} menu.
2. Select **Reorder floors and areas**.
3. In the dialog, move the floors or areas you want to rearrange:

    ![Reorder floors and areas](/images/organizing/reorder-areas-menu.png)
