This year’s challenge

Gather WTHs from the people you live with too. Ask them:

  • What annoys you most about Home Assistant?
  • Where do you get lost in the app?
  • When our home does something unexpected, do you know why?
  • What would you change yourself, if you knew how?

Then help them share their answers in their own words. Unfiltered feedback is worth its weight in gold!
