Four tips for a great WTH

  1. 1

    Check it’s not already there

    If it is, there’s no need to post again.

  2. 2

    Share one at a time

    So each can be discussed and followed on its own.

  3. 3

    Tell it like it is

    Explain the problem, we’ll figure out the fix.

  4. 4

    Be kind

    We’re all here because we care about Home Assistant, our homes and the people in them: that includes everyone on our forum!
