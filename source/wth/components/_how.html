How it works

Every WTH goes in the Month of “What the Heck?!” category on our community forum. It is open for the whole of November.

  1. 1

    One WTH per topic

    That way each one can be discussed, voted on, and tracked.

  2. 2

    The moment, not the fix

    Tell us what happened, what you expected, and why it bugged you.

  3. 3

    Search first, then vote

    Someone may have got there before you. Vote for theirs and add your story in a reply.

  4. 4

    Be kind

    That includes the people building Home Assistant, many of them volunteers in their spare time.
