You don’t have to post a WTH to take part. Picking up someone else’s and shipping a fix counts just as much, and plenty of them turn out to be small.

    Find one you can take on

    Reply in the topic first, so two people do not build the same thing twice.

  3. 3

    Open a pull request

    Link it back to the topic. Everyone following along then knows something is happening.
