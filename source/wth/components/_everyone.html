Run Home Assistant at home? Ask your household.

Not “is it working?”, because they will politely say yes. Ask them properly. You might not like all the answers, but there is a lot to learn in that conversation.

Post what you hear {% icon "mdi:arrow-right" %}
  • What annoys you most about using Home Assistant?
  • Can you find what you need in the app? Where do you get lost?
  • When the house does something unexpected, do you know why?
  • Is there anything you’d change yourself, but don’t dare to?
  • If you could change one thing, what would it be?

Better still, help them post it themselves, in their own words. Their words are exactly what we need.
