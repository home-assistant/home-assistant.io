Get the whole home talking

Home Assistant should work for everyone, not just whoever set it up. Find out if it does with a little gentle questioning:

  • What annoys you most about using Home Assistant?
  • Where do you get lost in the app?
  • When the house does something unexpected, do you know why?
  • What would you change yourself, if you knew how?
  • If you could change one thing, what would it be?

Then help them share their answers in their own words as a WTH.
