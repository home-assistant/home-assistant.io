## A community *sens*ation

Lewis’s videos were such a hit with Home Assistant DIYers and enthusiasts that in 2022 Everything Smart Home became [one of the first members](/blog/2022/04/29/creator-network-launched/) of our <a href="https://creators.home-assistant.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Home Assistant Creator Network</a>. That same year, Lewis and Dave, an electrical engineer, launched <a href="https://shop.everythingsmart.io" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Everything Smart Technology</a> to sell smart home gear they believe in (including official Home Assistant hardware from <a href="https://www.nabucasa.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Nabu Casa</a>!). But they didn’t stop there. Lewis also wanted to create devices he’d use in his own home, ones with features he couldn’t find elsewhere. And so the Everything Presence sensor range was born ⚒️

## Made ESPecially for you

All three Everything Presence sensors run <a href="https://esphome.io/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">ESPHome</a>, which lets them talk to Home Assistant directly over your home network, with no cloud connection required. They also carry the <a href="https://devices.esphome.io/made-for-esphome/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Made for ESPHome</a> badge: our guarantee that each device runs ESPHome out of the box, can be fully controlled without opening the case or soldering anything, and has its code made available for all to see.

Everything Smart Home take this responsibility to heart. Their <a href="https://github.com/EverythingSmartHome" target="_blank" rel="noopener">GitHub</a> is a true collective project where users suggest fixes, add features, and find inspiration for sensors of their own. And that commitment goes beyond code: Lewis even joined us at our Open Home Foundation Summit in Dublin last year to discuss what community members want from their tech. We love to see a partner build so openly for our community, and dedicate their time to keeping smart homes local and built to last.

Like every certified device in the program, the Everything Presence Lite, One, and Pro were tested by our in-house team for local control, privacy, and sustainability, and Everything Smart Home are dedicated to providing long-term support and firmware updates for all three. It’s a commitment that comes naturally: Lewis has plenty of experience using his YouTube channel to listen to users and improve the sensors based on their feedback, so we’ve no doubt the team will continue to bring that same care to our community.

So, what makes an Everything Presence sensor different from the motion sensor you might already have? Most motion sensors rely on passive infrared (PIR) alone, so if you sit still long enough, they assume you’ve left (cue: waving at the ceiling). Each Everything Presence sensor uses millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar, which picks up movements as small as breathing, and the One and Pro pair it with PIR for an instant response the moment you walk in. That means lights that stay on while you read, heating that only warms the rooms you’re in, and screens that switch off when you’ve wandered away. All three double as Bluetooth proxies, too, extending Home Assistant’s Bluetooth range around your home.

The devices certified to the program are:

- <a href="https://shop.everythingsmart.io/products/everything-presence-lite" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Everything Presence Lite</a> – A more affordable mmWave sensor that tracks up to three people across four zones you set up yourself, bringing multi-zone presence detection to every room.
- <a href="https://shop.everythingsmart.io/products/everything-presence-one-kit" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Everything Presence One</a> – Combines mmWave presence, PIR motion, temperature, humidity, and light sensing in one ESPHome device, covering everything a room automation needs from a single sensor.
- <a href="https://shop.everythingsmart.io/products/everything-presence-pro" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Everything Presence Pro</a> – A professional-grade sensor with two mmWave radars (long-range static and multi-target tracking), PIR motion and light sensing, and built for wired reliability in larger spaces and professional installs.

The most recent addition to the range, the Everything Presence Pro, stands out for pairing 802.3af Power over Ethernet (PoE) with Ethernet. This means that a single cable carries both power _and_ data, so installers can mount it in a ceiling like a Wi-Fi access point or security camera, with no Wi-Fi dropouts or power outlet to worry about.

## Everything, everywhere, all at once

Wi-Fi or wired, all three sensors do everything they need entirely within your home. But if you’d like to check in from afar, <a href="https://www.nabucasa.com/link/" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Home Assistant Link</a> (formerly Home Assistant Cloud) lets you see which rooms are occupied, or whether the heating’s still on in an empty house, from wherever you are. ## FAQs

Q: If I have a device that is not listed under Works with Home Assistant does this mean it’s not supported?

A: No! It just means that it hasn’t gone through a testing schedule with our team or doesn’t fit the requirements of the program. It might function perfectly well but be added to the testing schedule later down the road, or it might work under a different connectivity type that we don’t currently test under the program.

Q: OK, so what’s the point of the Works with program?

A: It highlights the devices we know work well with Home Assistant and the brands that make a long-term commitment to keeping support for these devices going. The certification agreement specifies that the devices must have the functionality you would expect within Home Assistant, operate locally without the need for cloud, and that they will continue to do so long-term.

Q: How were these devices tested?

A: Everything Smart Home’s certified devices were tested using Home Assistant over a standard Wi-Fi connection. If you have another setup that’s not a problem, but we test this way as it’s the most effective method for our team to certify within our ecosystem.

Q: Will you be adding more Everything Smart Home devices to the program?

A: Why not! We’re thrilled to foster a close relationship with the team at Everything Smart Home to work together on any upcoming releases or add in further products that are not yet listed here.
