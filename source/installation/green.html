Why Choose Home Assistant Green?

  • Energy Monitoring: Track your home's energy consumption in real-time

  • Local Control: Your data stays private - no cloud dependency required
  • Open Source: Fully customizable with endless integration possibilities

🔧 Hardware Specifications

Processor: Quad-core ARM Cortex-A7
RAM: 2GB LPDDR3
Storage: 32GB eMMC
Wireless: Wi-Fi 802.11ac, Bluetooth 5.0
Zigbee: Built-in coordinator
Ethernet: Gigabit Ethernet
Power: 5V USB-C
Dimensions: Compact and wall-mountable

Quick Start Guide

  1. Unbox & Power: Connect the USB-C power adapter and Ethernet cable
  2. Wait for Boot: The device will automatically start up (about 2-3 minutes)
  3. Connect: Use your phone or computer to access the setup wizard at http://homeassistant.local
  4. Configure: Follow the on-screen instructions to set up your account and Wi-Fi
  5. Add Devices: Start connecting your smart home devices and creating automations
